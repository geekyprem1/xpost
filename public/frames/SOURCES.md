# Phone mockup assets

Source: https://github.com/jonnyjackson26/device-frames-media

PNG files and corresponding screen coordinates are copied from device-frames-output/Apple iPhone: 17 Pro/Silver, 17 Pro Max/Cosmic Orange, 16/Black, Air/Space Black.

Legacy scene IDs are retained for saved project compatibility; visible labels reflect the actual devices.

## iPad and MacBook

- iPad Pro 11 and 13: https://github.com/jonnyjackson26/device-frames-media (M4 & M5, portrait Space Black and Silver). Screen coordinates from the accompanying template.json.
- MacBook Pro 14 and 16 Silver: https://github.com/jamesjingyi/mockup-device-frames (Exports/MacBook/MacBook Pro). Screen opening coordinates read from the original PNG alpha channel; assets are used unchanged.
